# PAYRUN01 pension rule verification

General research was checked 8 October 2026; the PFZW 2026 rules below were refreshed against the July 2026 regulation and January 2026 UPA guide on 9 October 2026. This note records source-backed rules and what remains unproven; it does not itself change or apply pension data.

## Company flat-premium arrangement

- For Lisa's post-WTP flat arrangement, Article 18a Wet op de loonbelasting sets a 30% fiscal ceiling for old-age pension and partner pension after retirement. The 2026 standard WTP franchise is €19,172. This is not Jaap's legacy Article 10aa / transition franchise. [Wet LB 1964, article 18a (2026)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002471/2026-01-01) · [CAP overview of 2026 AOW franchises](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-aow-franchises-en-aow-inbouwbedragen/)
- The 2026 maximum pensionable salary is €137,800. [Belastingdienst 2026 payroll-taxes figures](https://odb.belastingdienst.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Cijferbijlage-2026-bij-Nieuwsbrief-LH-LH-209-1B61FD_TG.pdf) · [Staatscourant 2025, 44282, confirming the 2026 cap](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-44282.html)
- The user-approved 15% company premium and 2/3 employer, 1/3 employee division are company-policy inputs, not statutory percentages. A 15% total is below the 30% fiscal ceiling, provided the arrangement uses the statutory pension base and the 15% includes the relevant pension premiums in scope. The data does not say whether costs, disability-cover premiums, or a WTP compensation premium are included; do not infer those from the 15% headline.

## Progressive reference tiers and grandfathering

- Jaap's user-approved legacy TEST reference is **Annex IV, Table 2** of the CAP Staffelsbesluit: a 1.701% middelloon basis, old-age-pension-only (OP-only) column, and 20.0% for age 60–64. The 20% is a reference-table rate for the specified legacy arrangement; Jaap's chosen employer/employee split (2/3 and 1/3) remains an explicitly synthetic company policy, not a generally mandated statutory split or rate. [CAP Staffelsbesluit, Annex IV Table 2 and conditions](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/staffelbesluit-pensioenen-26-juni-2023-gewijzigd-per-28-juni-2024/)

The 2026 Article 18ga salary cap is €137,800 and also applies to the transition regime. [CAP 2026 franchise and AOW amounts](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/overzicht-aow-franchises-en-aow-inbouwbedragen/) · [Staatscourant 2025, 44282, confirming the cap freeze at €137,800 for 2025–2026](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-44282.html)
- The Staffelsbesluit permits parties to use the worker's age at month-end for accrual and premium over the month. It is an allowed convention, not a universal default; the pension agreement must determine which convention applies. Under the supplied synthetic DOB, Jaap is 62 at 31 October 2026. [CAP Staffelsbesluit, section “Leeftijd”](https://centraalaanspreekpuntpensioenen.belastingdienst.nl/publicaties/staffelbesluit-pensioenen-26-juni-2023-gewijzigd-per-28-juni-2024/)

The 2025 amendment sets 31 December 2027 as the latest statutory participant-start date; that deadline is not the actual employer flat-premium effective date. [Pensioenwet, article 220e](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0020809/2023-07-01/0/Hoofdstuk6b) · [Staatsblad 2025, 423](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2025-423.html)

## Employee pension deduction and fiscal bases

- The 2026 Handboek Loonheffingen says an employee pension premium withheld from salary is entered in payroll record column 7. For a normally insured employee, employee-insurance wage, Zvw wage, and wage-tax/national-insurance wage are each based on columns 3 + 4 + 5 − 7. This supports deducting the employee contribution from each of those bases where the contribution qualifies as a pension premium; the bases remain distinct calculation concepts and must retain separate source/provenance. [Handboek Loonheffingen 2026, March edition, sections 11.2.6–11.2.9](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

## PFZW 2026

- **2026 applicability and amounts.** The July 2026 PFZW Pension Regulations (effective retroactively from 1 January 2026) govern the 2026 rules. Articles 5.1–5.6 set the FTE annual salary, €137,800 OP/NP cap, €17,283 OP/NP franchise, €79,409 WIA-excedent franchise, 25.9% base premium and 3.4% WIA-excedent premium. Kinderopvang-werkt records the 2026 CAO split as 12.9% employee / 13.0% employer for OP/NP and 0% employee / 3.4% employer for WIA-excedent. These values are not company-company pension assumptions. [PFZW Pension Regulations, July 2026, arts. 5.1–5.6](https://www.pfzw.nl/content/dam/pfzw/web/statuten-en-reglementen/Statuten%20en%20reglementen%20juli%202026.pdf) · [Kinderopvang-werkt 2026 premium decision](https://www.kinderopvang-werkt.nl/nieuws/pensioenpremies-2026)
- **Coverage gate.** A CAO label by itself does not prove a participant assignment or effective arrangement. In authorized Core/DEV source records, Frits has no effective pension assignment and no effective CAO-to-arrangement mapping. The engine requires both records and returns a source gap; no enrollment or zero-premium fallback was created.
- **Salary basis.** PFZW art. 5.1.1–2 sets the full-time structural annual salary including holiday pay and structural year-end allowance at participation start and each 1 January (or the first day of the first four-week period). Article 5.2.4 determines whether the EJU is structural, its rate and eligible salary components from the preceding 31 December. For 2026, the CAO EJU rate was 5.5% on 31 December 2025 and increased to 8% from 1 January 2026; that increase does not replace the explicit PFZW prior-year reference. The UPA guide describes monthly full-time salary ×12 (×13 for four-week payroll), qualifying structural components, holiday pay and the prior-year EJU; `RegLn` is rounded UP to whole euros. For Frits, applying 5.5% to a €41,100 annual salary base is only a preview input: his 2025 eligible salary components are not evidenced in Core. [PFZW Regulations, arts. 5.1–5.2](https://www.pfzw.nl/content/dam/pfzw/web/statuten-en-reglementen/Statuten%20en%20reglementen%20juli%202026.pdf) · [PFZW UPA 2026 guide, pp. 9–10](https://www.pfzw.nl/content/dam/pfzw/web/werkgevers/pensioenaangifte/2026_Handleiding-aanlevering-UPA-gegevens.pdf) · [CAO Kinderopvang, art. 5.7](https://www.kinderopvang-werkt.nl/cao-kinderopvang-2025-2026/eindejaarsuitkering)
- **Part-time and additional hours.** PFZW art. 5.3 defines DTF as hours worked ÷ full-time hours for the employee's group, separately per employment, and permits a DTF above 1. The UPA guide specifies 156 period hours for a 36-hour workweek (p. 19), `PtPerc` rounded to two percentage decimals with the third decimal half-up (p. 31), and extra worked hours increased by the applicable ADV/holiday-days percentage (p. 19). Its exact employer/CAO uplift percentage must be supplied; a general CAO label or a calculation from leave entitlement is not enough. UPA includes pensionable hours each period; ordinary months do not vary with SV days, while extra actual hours can change that period's hours. [PFZW Regulations, art. 5.3](https://www.pfzw.nl/content/dam/pfzw/web/statuten-en-reglementen/Statuten%20en%20reglementen%20juli%202026.pdf) · [PFZW UPA 2026 guide, pp. 19–20 and 31](https://www.pfzw.nl/content/dam/pfzw/web/werkgevers/pensioenaangifte/2026_Handleiding-aanlevering-UPA-gegevens.pdf)
- **Premium bases and WIA conditions.** OP/NP base is `max(min(RegLn, €137,800) − €17,283, 0) × period DTF`. WIA-excedent has a separate base `max(RegLn × period DTF − €79,409, 0)` and is not subject to the OP/NP cap. Article 11.5 identifies WIA-excedent coverage only when the pensionable salary × DTF exceeds the maximum WIA annual wage; the employer premium therefore remains zero below that threshold. Frits's preview salary is far below the WIA threshold, so no WIA premium is included.
- **Period allocation and cents.** PFZW requires period-level UPA hours and says premium is added monthly; PFZW's UPA FAQ says prior-period corrections appear on a later invoice. That confirms corrections belong to the affected prior period, not to a later month's ordinary hours. The sources do not specify a payroll deduction's exact monthly cents allocation sequence or establish that PAYRUN01 should calculate annual shares / 12 and round employee and employer shares separately. That part is an employer-payroll policy gate. The local preview uses exact decimals and HALF_UP cents per share, reconciles the 12.9% + 13.0% split, and remains `PREVIEW_ONLY` until the employer's documented policy and effective version are supplied. Mid-period participation proration is likewise not assumed; this implementation blocks such a case until its rule is sourced. [PFZW UPA FAQ](https://www.pfzw.nl/werkgevers/pensioenaangifte/faq.html)
- **Fiscal and employer-cost integration.** The 2026 Handboek Loonheffingen column-7 pension deduction reduces the applicable LB/PH, SV and Zvw bases when the pension payment qualifies; PAYRUN01 keeps the bases independently represented. Employee share reduces employee net, employer share increases employer cost, and employer premium never reduces employee net. [Handboek Loonheffingen 2026, §§11.2.6–11.2.9](https://download.belastingdienst.nl/belastingdienst/docs/handboek-loonheffingen-lh0221t61fd.pdf)

The reproducible Frits arithmetic preview and the exact missing evidence are recorded in [PAY-RULE-002 PFZW 2026 acceptance](../acceptance/PAY-RULE-002-PFZW-2026-20261009.md). The preview is not a persisted payroll result or an official UPA submission.
